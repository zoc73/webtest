About the Novel

"Lord of the Mysteries" is a Western fantasy masterpiece by Chinese author "Cuttlefish That Loves Diving." Serialized on Qidian from 2018 to 2020, totaling roughly 4.47 million words, it is widely regarded as a landmark work in online literature.

Set in a 19th-century Victorian-esque world where the Industrial Revolution reshapes civilization while ancient mystical forces stir in the shadows. Protagonist Zhou Mingrui transmigrates into Klein Moretti, stepping into the world of Beyonders guided by occult notes and the mysterious "Sefirah Castle."

The novel blends Lovecraftian horror, SCP containment concepts, Tarot symbolism, and steampunk aesthetics into a vast, self-consistent world.
