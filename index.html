▍About the Novel

"Lord of the Mysteries" is a Western fantasy masterpiece by Chinese author "Cuttlefish That Loves Diving." Serialized on Qidian from April 1, 2018 to May 1, 2020, totaling roughly 4.47 million words, it is widely regarded as a landmark work in online literature.

Set in an alternate world resembling 19th-century Victorian era — where the Industrial Revolution of steam and machinery reshapes civilization, while ancient mystical forces stir in the shadows. Protagonist Zhou Mingrui transmigrates into the body of Klein Moretti, a history student who committed suicide. Guided by occult notes and the mysterious "Sefirah Castle," he steps into the world of Beyonders.

The novel blends Lovecraftian horror, SCP Foundation containment concepts, Tarot symbolism, and steampunk aesthetics into a vast, self-consistent world. Its rigorous sequence system, intellectual confrontations, and deep character arcs make it an indispensable classic.

▍Core Concepts
22 Beyonder Pathways

Each pathway governs a domain of authority, from Seq 9 to Seq 0 (10 stages). The three Mystery pathways (Seer, Thief, Apprentice) converge into the Lord of the Mysteries.

Sefirah Castle

One of the three Great Aspects, the dwelling of the Lord of Mysteries. The owner can host Gatherings above the Gray Fog, blocking all scrying and divination.

Beyonder Char. Convergence

Characteristics of the same pathway inevitably converge. High-level beings unconsciously attract related characteristics — a gravitational law at the level of fate.

Four Sacred Incantations

"Fusheng Xuanhuang Tianzun, Shangdi, Xianjun, Daojun" — corresponding to the four Great Aspects. Charms seal Beyonder powers into paper for low-sequence combat.

Sealed Artifacts

Beyonder characteristics in objects form Sealed Artifacts (Level 0-3). Each has unique contamination mechanics. Level 0 artifacts rival Angel-level beings.

Steampunk Setting

Victorian-esque world where Loen, Intis, Feysac vie for power. Steam locomotives and newspapers coexist with Beyonder arts. Seven orthodox gods rule.

▍Secret Organizations

⛤ Tarot Gathering

Klein's secret society above the Gray Fog. Members: Justice (Audrey), Hanged Man (Alger), Star (Leonard), Sun (Derrick), Moon (Emlyn), Hermit (Cattleya), Judgment (Xio).

⛤ Aurora Order

Fanatics worshipping the "True Creator." Global intelligence network, hostile to all churches. Uses sacrifice and violence to pursue divine revelation.

⛤ Psychology Alchemists

Spectator-pathway secret society. Masters of psychological manipulation and dream control, pulling strings behind politics and major events.

⛤ Church of Evernight

Secret order serving the Evernight Goddess. Operates through Nighthawks and Red Gloves, protecting Backlund's hidden order.

⛤ Life School of Thought

Radical offshoot of the Steam Church devoted to knowledge. Deeply researches Beyonder characteristics, their library holds vast forbidden knowledge.

⛤ City of Silver · Moon City

Settlements in the God-Forsaken Land. Silver City struggles in eternal darkness. Moon City's residents are The Fool's most loyal congregation.
