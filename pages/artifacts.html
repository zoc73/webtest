Sealed Artifacts & Mystical Items

◈ Lv.0 · Phantom Thief Gloves

Sealed Artifact · Level 0

Steals objects and even concepts. Self-aware, requires feeding of Beyonder characteristics.

◈ Lv.0 · Magic Mirror

Sealed Artifact · Level 0

A mirror holding a Seq 1 soul. Answers questions in metaphor, exacting a price.

◈ Lv.1 · Auto-Wishing Machine

Sealed Artifact · Level 1

Coin-operated. More wishes = worse luck. Klein cleverly exploited it.

◈ Lv.1 · Door of No Source

Sealed Artifact · Level 1

A door connecting anywhere. Requires spiritual judgment.

◈ Antigonus Notebook

Mystical Item

Contains Seq 1 Mystery Pryer's characteristic. Klein's most important early acquisition.

◈ Wishing Lamp

Mystical Item

Sealed with an ancient being. Grants 3 wishes, each with terrible side effects.

◈ Creeping Hunger

Sealed Artifact · Level 1

A glove that can devour and use Beyonder abilities. Klein's signature equipment during his early adventures.

◈ Staff of the Sea

Sealed Artifact · Level 2

Controls weather and sea. Key artifact of the Sailor pathway.

Key Locations

◈ Backlund

Capital of Loen Kingdom

The main setting of the story. A sprawling metropolis divided into districts: Cherwood Borough, Hillston Borough, Queen's Quarter, etc.

◈ Sefirah Castle

Above the Gray Fog

The dwelling of the Lord of Mysteries. A castle above the gray fog where Klein holds Tarot Gatherings.

◈ City of Silver

Forsaken Land of God

A settlement of survivors in eternal darkness. Led by Sun pathway Beyonders.

◈ Tingen

Where It All Began

The small city where Klein first awakened as a Beyonder. Home to the Nighthawks headquarters.
